# Project Charter

## Mission

Make linear algebra easier to *see* without making it harder to follow the course.

The MATH 1554 Visual Companion provides short, original, open-source mathematical animations that follow the sequence of Georgia Tech's MATH 1554 course notes and connect geometric intuition directly to the symbolic work students are expected to perform.

## Problem statement

Linear algebra has two common failure modes for learners:

1. symbolic procedures are learned without a durable geometric model;
2. excellent visual explanations are available, but their sequencing and notation do not match the student's course.

This project addresses the second problem without sacrificing the first.

## Primary users

- students currently taking linear algebra;
- TAs and instructors who want short visual explanations;
- contributors learning Manim through mathematically meaningful work;
- educators adapting visuals to other linear algebra courses.

## Non-goals

This project is not intended to:

- reproduce or replace Georgia Tech's course notes;
- reproduce 3Blue1Brown videos or creative assets;
- become a complete textbook;
- optimize for cinematic animation at the expense of mathematical clarity;
- encode one instructor's exact lecture script;
- require contributors to own proprietary software.

## Quality bar

A merged scene should be:

- mathematically correct;
- visually legible at 1080p;
- understandable without relying on color alone;
- focused on one primary concept;
- connected to an explicit learning objective;
- rendered from original code and original examples;
- documented well enough for another contributor to maintain it.

## Success criteria

The project is succeeding when a student can move from the course notes to a matching visualization in seconds, understand *why* a computation works, and return to the symbolic material with a clearer mental model.
